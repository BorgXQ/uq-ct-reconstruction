How should I interpret these intervals?
    
Gaussian measurement noise passes through the fixed linear reconstruction, giving Γₖ = σ²TₖTₖᵀ. SD describes random variability; it excludes systematic reconstruction bias.

    
A declared source class supplies a shared bias allowance Bₖ. Each raw interval is reconstructed density ± (z SD + Bₖ). Missing source assumptions leave total-error intervals unavailable. The allowance bounds image-wide bias and can be conservative at one pixel.

    
Under valid source assumptions and noise-independent settings, coverage is marginal for each fixed pixel across repeated measurements. It is not a simultaneous whole-image guarantee or a posterior probability about a fixed observed interval. Retrospective choices are exploratory; declaring independence does not prove it.

    
First-crossing stopping limits noise amplification while allowing bias to decrease. It can fail within the budget and is not an optimizer of image error or interval width.

    
GFI considers Qᵧᴷ(U) = Tₖ(y − σU), with observed y fixed and auxiliary Gaussian U. Its covariance matches Γₖ, but repeated-measurement sampling varies Y and has mean TₖAx†. No sampler, extra measurement noise or Bayesian posterior is computed here.

    
Full-spectrum UQ supports 16×16 and 32×32 under resource limits. 64×64 remains available for M2 reconstruction. Joint inverse-covariance regions are not implemented; eligibility reasons are reported. Randomized methods and repeated coverage experiments belong to later milestones.
