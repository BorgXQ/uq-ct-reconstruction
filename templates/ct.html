What is this scan measuring?
Each measurement sums frozen numerical pixel densities weighted by the ray’s intersection length. One independent Gaussian perturbation is drawn per measurement. Its absolute SD is in line-integral units, not a percentage. The observation remains fixed throughout reconstruction and iteration inspection. Zero noise creates an ordinary observation equal to the clean sinogram.

What does Landweber do?
Starting from zero, xₖ₊₁ = xₖ + ω Aᵀ(y − Axₖ). Each update predicts measurements, applies the actual matrix transpose to the residual, and takes a fixed-size correction. The solver uses only the operator and observed measurements.
A smaller measurement residual need not mean a smaller image error. Missing acquisition information can leave multiple images compatible with the same measurements. Inspecting earlier iterations is exploratory; automatic stopping and uncertainty begin in M3.
This simulator and solver use the same discrete operator. The experiment isolates numerical/noise behavior; it does not test real-scanner modeling mismatch or provide medically calibrated results.
